08 / Motion

REACT.
DON'T PERFORM.

Motion confirms hierarchy, state change, or pointer presence. Background fields may react subtly to the cursor. Primary actions may shift a few pixels. Dense surfaces remain stable. Avoid floating blobs, perpetual card bobbing, glassy hover tricks, or animation that makes finance feel less trustworthy. Reduced-motion preferences remain first-class.
