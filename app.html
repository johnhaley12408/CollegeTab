03 / FINANCING STACK

FEDERAL FIRST. PRIVATE ONLY FOR THE GAP.

CollegeTab models every obligation separately. Direct Subsidized loans do not accrue interest while enrolled at least half-time or during the standard six-month grace period. Direct Unsubsidized and deferred private loans begin accruing from disbursement.

CURRENT-LAW FEDERAL BASELINETIERED STANDARDFor modeled Direct Loans first disbursed on or after July 1, 2026, CollegeTab automatically uses the fixed-payment Tiered Standard term based on the borrower's Direct principal balance: 10, 15, 20, or 25 years. Parent PLUS is calculated on the parent's separate balance sheet.
PRIVATE REPAYMENT BEHAVIORAPR + ORIGINATION FEE ARE ENTERED FOR EACH ACADEMIC-YEAR TRANCHE BELOW.
ANNUAL BORROWING PLANGross amounts borrowed. Origination fees reduce what reaches the school.

ANNUAL LOAN PRICING + FEE ASSUMPTIONSEDIT INPUTS →

For 2026–27, undergraduate Direct Subsidized/Unsubsidized loans use a 6.52% fixed rate and Parent PLUS uses 9.07%. Current federal origination-fee inputs are 1.057% for Direct Subsidized/Unsubsidized and 4.228% for PLUS. Future federal award-year values remain editable planning proxies. Private APR and origination fee are entered separately for each academic-year tranche from the lender's actual terms; enter 0 explicitly when a private loan has no fee.

How does our savings model work?OPEN →

1. Choose pre-tax 401(k).
The maximum is the lower of the annual legal limit and what still lets modeled take-home cover expenses and required loan payments.

2. Recalculate taxes.
CollegeTab deducts the selected traditional 401(k) contribution before federal and eligible state income-tax calculations. FICA still applies.

3. Protect the emergency fund.
Post-tax cash fills the annually repriced emergency HYSA target before optional post-tax destinations.

4. Use the remaining dollars.
HSA, Roth IRA and brokerage sliders receive dynamic annual-dollar maximums. Cash/HYSA automatically receives anything left.
